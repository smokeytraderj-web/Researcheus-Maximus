Import from J.P. Morgan Markets

Two ways to bring a house view across. The extension is the one to install: after it, typing a ticker and pressing Generate Research is the whole workflow — the rating, price target, equity profile and forward estimates arrive on their own, refreshed on every run.

The extension — nothing to click

Recommended
  1. Open chrome://extensions in this browser.
  2. Turn on Developer mode, top right.
  3. Press Load unpacked and choose the extension folder inside the project.
  4. Reload this app. That is the whole setup.

From then on: type a ticker, press Generate Research, read the report. Nothing else.

Type a ticker rather than a company name where you can. AAPL resolves to one security; “Apple” also matches Apple Hospitality REIT, and rather than guess between them the import stands down and the report says the view was unavailable.

Or the bookmarklet — one click, no install

A button for your bookmarks bar. On a company page it reads the same figures and hands them here for checking. Useful without the extension, and it also brings across the analyst and the latest note when you are already on the page.

Drag this to your bookmarks bar ↧  Import to Analyst Agent

Dragging is the only step — clicking it here does nothing, because there is no company page to read. Press Ctrl + Shift + B if your bookmarks bar is hidden.

  1. Sign in to J.P. Morgan Markets as you normally do, in this browser.
  2. Open the company page for the security you are researching.
  3. Click Import to Analyst Agent in your bookmarks bar.
  4. This app opens in a new tab with the figures filled in. Check them, correct anything wrong, and save.

What it sends, and what it does not

It sends the figures: the rating, the target, the profile rows, the house's estimates, and the text of the page for the analyst name and the latest note. It sends nothing else. No password, cookie, token or session detail is read or forwarded — and the server refuses any import that arrives carrying something shaped like one, rather than quietly cleaning it up.

You stay signed in under your own entitlement, in your own browser, reading a page already open in front of you. This app holds no credential for the portal and needs none.

With the bookmarklet nothing is recorded until you press save. The extension records the view as part of the run, without that step — the figures come across already typed and labelled by the portal, so there is no parse to check. Either way they are stored as published, attributed to the house, and shown with the date they carry; a rating stays in that house's own words. A view the portal did not date is not stored at all, because an undated rating reads as a current one.

If the button does not work, the page will say why. You can always paste the page instead — that route is unchanged.
