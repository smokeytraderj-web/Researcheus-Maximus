Are your strongest stocks moving together?

01 / Return vs. score

Higher = stronger technical score. Further right = a larger gain over the past year. Color marks correlation groups; larger points have higher scores. Similar scores or yearly gains alone do not mean stocks are correlated.

02 / 100-day vs. 200-day return

Each point is a stock. Further right = a larger gain over the last 100 trading days; higher = a larger gain over the last 200. Nearby stocks have similar performance over these windows. Colors identify groups confirmed by daily-return correlation.

The last 100 days are part of the 200-day window. This overlap can create a strong relationship between the two axes; nearby points alone do not prove that stocks move together day to day.

Groups require every member pair to have correlation ≥0.75; Remove market effect compares residual daily returns after a separate regression of each stock on SPY. Correlations use up to 252 common daily returns, with at least 126 required. Both plots show actual stock returns, even when the market-effect toggle changes the correlation groups. Returns use trading days, not calendar days. Historical correlation does not establish causation or predict future diversification.

How scores are calculated

Each stock receives the same independent, rules-based score. Scores run from 1.0 to 10.0; they are technical setup assessments, not return probabilities.

Trend 30%Momentum 20%Relative strength 20%Entry / risk 20%Volume 10%

Trend: position and slope of the 50/200-day averages. Momentum: RSI and MACD histogram. Relative strength: 21/63-session excess return versus SPY. Entry/risk: extension above the 20-day average, distance to a support-based stop reference, and ATR. Volume: 20-session directional volume balance. Component values are 0–100; final score = 1 + 9 × weighted components / 100. Yahoo adjusted daily history is preferred, with a verified Nasdaq fallback when needed. Each row identifies its provider and price basis; stock and SPY comparison always use the same adjustment basis. Corporate actions may affect unadjusted fallback indicators. The latest bar may be intraday. RSI uses exponentially smoothed gains/losses after 14 observations.

8–10: strong setup · 6–7.9: constructive · 4–5.9: mixed · below 4: weak. At least 220 sessions, recent data, usable volume, and aligned SPY history are required. Missing data receives no score. The support/stop reference is a chart measure, not an order recommendation. Scores ignore valuation, earnings events, and portfolio suitability.
